# Submission files for Forestry

Prepared 2026-10-19 for the ScholarOne site of Forestry, An International Journal of Forest Research, at https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/forest. Every requirement below is quoted from the journal's General Instructions as saved by Seamus on 2026-10-19 in `04.references/General Instructions | Forestry ... .pdf`, and the file that meets it is named beside it. Every file here is a copy made by the render of 2026-10-19; the sources are `01.manuscript/manuscript.qmd`, `supplementary.qmd`, `cover_letter.qmd` and `title_page.qmd`, and a file edited here by hand will be overwritten by the next render.

| File | What the instructions ask | Where it is met |
|---|---|---|
| `manuscript.docx` | "Submissions should be double-spaced throughout (including references), leaving a 2.5 cm margin at the left, top and bottom. Pages should be numbered and lines also numbered consecutively from the start. A4-size paper is preferred, and a font size no smaller than 11 should be used." "Tables ... should be numbered in arabic numerals, typed on separate pages and collected at the end of the text. Their approximate position in the text should be indicated in the page margin." "A well-written abstract of no more than 300 words." "Please include each alt text description in your main manuscript file, directly under the figure legend." A Funding section, a conflict of interest statement, a Data Availability Statement, and a Supplementary material section "placed immediately before the Acknowledgements section" | The main document, code hidden, Times New Roman 11 pt, double spaced, line and page numbered, A4 with 2.5 cm margins; the six tables on their own pages after the References, the wide ones landscape; the two figures after them with captions and alt text; a Word comment in the margin reading "Table 1 near here" or "Figure 1 near here" at the paragraph each followed; the abstract at 296 words; the end matter in the order Supplementary material, Funding, Conflict of interest, Author contributions, Data availability statement, References. |
| `figure1.tif`, `figure2.tif` | "Figures must be saved in separate files ... Files should be named according to DOS conventions, e.g. 'figure1.tif'." "For bitmapped graphics, TIFF is the preferred format." "500 d.p.i. for colour and half-tone artwork." "Colour figures must have a resolution of at least 300 dots per inch at their final size." | Each figure as a TIFF at 500 dots per inch at its printed width of 7.5 inches, drawn by the manuscript's own code. |
| `tables.docx` | The tables as above, offered as a separate file for the upload form; they are also at the end of `manuscript.docx` | Tables 1 to 6 with their captions, each on its own page, the wide ones landscape, 11 pt. |

| `cover_letter.docx` | "Accompanying information must confirm that all authors have read and approved the manuscript and give the names, addresses, telephone/fax numbers, and e-mail addresses of four potential referees. The four referees, two outside Canada, with addresses, telephone numbers and e-mail addresses, are not in any file and must be entered by Seamus in the form or added to this letter. |
